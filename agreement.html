Investor Agreement

Please read the following agreement carefully before creating your investment account.

1. Capital Withdrawal

I understand that my investment capital cannot be withdrawn until after six (6) months from my first investment.

2. Investment Duration

Each investment cycle lasts for fifteen (15) days from the investment activation date.

3. Package Upgrade

I agree to upgrade my investment package every two (2) months to remain eligible for future withdrawals.

4. Referral Bonus

Money Rain pays a one-time referral commission of five percent (5%) on every successfully activated referral.

5. Banking Details

The bank account used during registration must belong to me and must match my registered name.

6. Deposit Policy

All investment deposits must be made using the same bank account submitted during registration.

7. Password Recovery

I understand that a ₦3,000 administrative fee may apply for account recovery if I lose my login credentials, subject to the platform's current recovery policy.

8. Platform Communication

I agree to follow the official Money Rain WhatsApp Channel to receive company announcements and investment updates.

9. Compliance

I understand that providing false information may result in suspension or permanent closure of my investment account.
